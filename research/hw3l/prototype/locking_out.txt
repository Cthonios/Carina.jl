Assembled soft-mode and locking sweep, kappa/mu = 1e4.

  rank(G)   volumetric directions the pressure space constrains
  isochoric n_u - rank(G): deformations still available as kappa -> inf
            -> collapses toward zero = volumetric locking
  n_zero    assembled modes with no energy at all
  lam_min   smallest eigenvalue, relative to the deviatoric scale

Two boundary conditions.  `all` fixes the whole boundary, the standard
inf-sup setting and the most constraining a mesh can have.  `zface`
fixes only z = 0, which is far closer to the confined-but-not-clamped
conditions under which soft modes are reported.

  iso frac cannot fall below 1 - n_p/n_u; a pair that ATTAINS that
            bound spends every pressure degree of freedom on a distinct
            displacement direction and wastes none.

Enriched pair, assembled rather than extrapolated.

An earlier version of this sweep did not build the bubble.  It added
3*nelem to n_u and carried rank(G) over unchanged, on the reasoning
that the bubble vanishes on the element boundary.  That reasoning is
sound for P0 and wrong for P1disc: the bubble divergence integrates to
zero against a CONSTANT pressure, so it adds nothing to the P0 rows,
but it pairs nonzero with the linear ones.  Those columns can raise
rank(G), and every count downstream of it, so the pair is assembled
here and the extrapolated figure is reported beside it.
