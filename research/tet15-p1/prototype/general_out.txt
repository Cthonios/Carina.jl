plastic J2 model, whose stress is not the derivative of its Helmholtz energy during flow.

the model's stress by 2e-5 to 5e-2 in its symmetric part, and the pointwise element without
projection shows the same difference (0.13).  Reduction (a) below shows that the general
element reproduces the split element's tangent to rounding, so the discrepancy is the
material's and not the element's.
