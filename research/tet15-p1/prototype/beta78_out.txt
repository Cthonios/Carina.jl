Is P2/P0 uniformly inf-sup stable?

beta_h is the smallest NONZERO singular value of M^(-1/2) G L^(-T),
with the null dimension fixed independently as n_p - rank(G) so the
cut is selected by index rather than by a threshold.

A uniformly stable pair has beta_h flattening as h -> 0 (rate ~ 0).
A rate near 0.5 over a widening range is a genuine decay.

The interior bubble cannot affect a P0 pressure.  Its divergence
integrates to zero against any constant, so its columns of G vanish
identically and it acts only through enlarging K -- which can raise
beta_h but never lower it.  P2+interior/P0 sitting just above P2/P0
with the same null dimension is that statement, measured; the wiring
check itself is checks.jl, which reads those columns of G directly.
